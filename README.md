# Amazon India Sales Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard built for a management audience (non-technical) to track
sales, profit, products, orders, payments, fulfilment and state performance.

## Dataset
10,000 orders from Jan 2024 to Aug 2026 across 5 categories, 25 products,
10 states, 5 payment methods and 3 fulfilment types.

## Dashboard pages
1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance
2. Category & Product Performance
3. Order Status & Revenue Loss
4. Payment, Fulfilment & Geographic Performance

## Key findings
- Total sales ₹155.79M, profit ₹33.17M, profit margin 21.29%
- Electronics & Mobiles drives most of sales and profit
- About 10% of orders are lost to returns and cancellations (₹15.65M)
- UPI is about 50% of sales; Amazon FBA handles about 69%
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and Delhi are the top markets

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query

## How to open
Download `dashboard/project1sales.pbix` and open it in Power BI Desktop.
